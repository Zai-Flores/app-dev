# my favorite game
## Assassin's Creed game series

**Assassin's Creed** - a game made by *ubisoft*; An open world game where one plays as an elite member of a secret society of Assassins during a prehistorical era. Whether it’s the Crusades(AC I), the Renaissance(AC II, AC brotherhood), or the Golden Age of Piracy(AC IV), the hook is the same: you meet real historical figures and "participate" in major events.

What I'm currently playing: 
**Assassin's Creed Unity** (my fave :heart_eyes_cat: )

Game description: 
```
Setting: Paris, France during the French Revolution.

Time Period: Primarily 1789 – 1794 (The fall of the Bastille to the execution of Robespierre).

Main Character: Arno Victor Dorian
Arno is a witty, somewhat cynical protagonist whose journey is driven by personal redemption rather than blind devotion to the Assassin Creed.

Background: Born to an Assassin father but raised by a Templar Grandmaster (his father’s friend) after being orphaned.

Personality: He is charismatic and impulsive, often breaking the rules of the Brotherhood to follow his own moral compass.

Motivation: His primary drive is his love for Elise de la Serre (a Templar) and his guilt over failing to prevent the deaths of the two father figures in his life.


Plot: The plot is set in a fictional history of real-world events and follows the millennia-old struggle between the Assassins, who fight to preserve peace and free will, and the Templars, who desire peace through control.
The main story is set in Paris during the French Revolution from 1789 to 1794, and follows Assassin Arno Dorian and his efforts to expose the true powers behind the Revolution, while seeking revenge against those responsible for his adoptive father's murder.
```
